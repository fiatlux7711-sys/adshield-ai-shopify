# Free beta release policy

- Price: free; no Shopify subscription or usage charge is created.
- Scope: `read_products` only.
- Default scan cap: 50 recently updated products per manual run.
- Automatic edits: none.
- AI provider: disabled by default.
- A pass is not a compliance certification and does not replace legal review.
- Merchants can uninstall at any time and request earlier export or deletion through support.
- Paid plans must not be introduced until pricing, trial, cancellation, refund, entitlement, listing disclosure, and Shopify Billing API behavior are implemented and tested together.
