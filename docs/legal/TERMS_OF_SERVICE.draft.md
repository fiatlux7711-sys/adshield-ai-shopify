## 1. The service

AdShield AI ("the App") is a Shopify application that screens product marketing
copy for **potential advertising-compliance risk** and returns a prioritized
review list with the matched text, an explanation, and suggested next actions.

not provide legal advice, and does not certify legal compliance.**
